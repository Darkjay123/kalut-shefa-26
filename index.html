The name of the day

Kalut-Shefa

✦

Two Hebrew words, one promise for the life they are building together.

קלות

Kalut ease

A love that feels light to carry. Grace in the ordinary days, peace in the hard ones, laughter that comes easily.

שפע

Shefa abundance

Overflowing joy, open doors, full tables, and more than enough love to share with everyone who walked with them here.

“Ease and abundance.” That is the wish, the theme, and the whole mood of these two weekends.
